# Skill — ZPD Validator

Fail-closed checklist. The **Validator Bot** (`validator.md`) owns critique. This file is the floor; the Bot’s live-page method is the ceiling. If this skill would PASS a Chatswood-style template-2-on-wellbeing email, the Bot still kills it.

## When to use

Before showing Jacques any Facebook post, outreach email, partnership email, Google post, ad copy, or prospect list.

## Checks — fail closed

A pack **fails** if any check fails. Do not send, post, or “fix quietly.” Return the scorecard and rewrite only the failing pieces.

### 1. Voice (`positioning.md`)

- Sounds like pickup-line parent talk, Australian English
- Uses **you** / **your child** — not families, learners, kid(s)
- None of the kill-list: “something we wish more parents knew”, “hot take”, “plain-language guide”, “by design”, “curriculum-native”, “we don’t promise magic grades”, therapist hedging, template closers on every email

Read aloud test: would Jacques say this to a friend’s face?

### 2. CTA

- Default CTA is **Diagnostic ($110)** or the **guide** (`https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit`)
- Do not lead with Essential ($1,050) unless the task says so
- Cold email and first-touch partnership: the **guide** (`https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit`), not `/enrol`. T5/T4 also get the newsletter PNG (inline + attach). T3 also gets the PDF. A PNG cannot hold a click — fail if the URL is dropped because “the image is enough.”
- From address is **grow@zpdlearning.com** via **Zoho Mail**. Fail if the client is Gmail or From/Reply-To is `jacquescaspar@gmail.com`
- GROW50 only in comments / when asked / paid Ad C — not in cold email body

### 3. Channel rule

### 4. Audience

- Default audience = parents, Years 5–12, **anywhere in Australia** (online or in-home). Localise senior-cert (HSC/VCE/QCE/SACE/WACE/TCE) to the target’s state. Fail copy that assumes only Sydney.
- Tutor copy must not appear in parent channels
- Do not target children. Do not imply under-16s should be contacted.

### 5. Platform and this pack (`PLAYBOOK.md`)

- Facebook: each post’s group is eligible (2/month, 24h after join); no identical body in two groups the same day
- Fail un-reviewed burst send; fail new platforms / Phase 2 product / invented contacts
- Do **not** fail a pack for being large. National volume is expected. Fail hookless rows and empty-state neglect (Sydney-only while other states are empty on `coverage.md`)

### 6. Source

- Facebook posts come from `02-facebook-posts.md` (adapt, don’t replace)
- Emails use templates in `03-outreach-emails.md` as bones (T1–T5 first-touch; follow-up templates last)
- Line 1 is paraphrased, not a dumped H1 or “you invite ideas”
- Guide URL in every **A** first-touch; B may put the item in the body instead (paste block / PDF handout / short piece)
- Never “Parent's Guide to ZPD” on school/P&C
- T5/T4: newsletter PNG. T3: PDF. Variant A or B from Prospects — 50/50 per type, do not reshuffle
- P&C/newsletter → template 4 + president/comms, not admin.officer
- Template 2 never on wellbeing-only walls or paid tutor directories
- Prospects: URL + why + actual template + right To: in `tracking/outreach-log.md`
- No invented names, emails, or metrics; coverage must not ignore empty states
