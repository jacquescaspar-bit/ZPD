# Outreach Emails — 6 templates

**From:** grow@zpdlearning.com (Zoho only — never Gmail)

**Guide:** https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit  
**Print/paste pack:** `guide-newsletter-paste.md` — newsletter PNG + PDF. A PNG cannot hold a click; the URL in the body (or an HTML wrap) is the link.  
Never enrol, never GROW50, in cold email.

**Bones, not paste.** Personalise with a **paraphrased** on-page detail (not the dumped H1). Open as a person (`I'm Jacques from ZPD Learning`) then a soft ask — not a three-line command. **A** is the ask + URL. **B** is paste-ready (the item is already in the email). Same opening. Split **50/50 per type** (slug A–Z; first half A, extra odd to A). Variant lives on the Prospects sheet — do not shuffle at send time.

**Volume:** national. Operator drafts every qualified ready row. Validator critiques. Jacques batch-approves.

**Log:** `tracking/outreach-log.md` (state/city). Coverage: `tracking/coverage.md`.

### Who they are, what they get (the ask must match)

Synergy is not an incentive. Name what they get.

| Type                  | Their job                     | Our first-touch ask                                          | Why they might say yes                                                   | Pay?                                                                                                              |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **T5 school office**  | Door to the parent newsletter | **Get this into the newsletter**                             | Direct line to that school’s parents.                                    | Do **not** offer to pay on first-touch. If they send a rate card, paying is fine. At scale, unpaid T4 is cheaper. |
| **T4 P&C / P&F**      | Community notes to members    | **Unpaid parent page** for members already weighing tutoring | Their members _are_ those parents. This is the main way to avoid paying. | Avoid if we can. Not the school’s ad book.                                                                        |
| **T3 clinic / SPELD** | Therapy / infoline            | **Handout, no fee** when the parent asks about tutoring      | They get asked every week and should not have to name a company.         | No.                                                                                                               |
| **T1 blog**           | Fill an Educate section       | **Link or short piece**                                      | They need copy readers will finish.                                      | No (editorial).                                                                                                   |
| **T2 resource list**  | Curate parent/education links | **Add one link**                                             | Completeness. Low effort.                                                | No.                                                                                                               |

Keep: line 1 from the page, no `/enrol`, no GROW50. Guide URL on T1–T4. T5 may include the guide as the page the slot would point to.

---

### Quality bar (fail the draft, not “close enough”)

- Line 1 proves you opened the tab. Fail “Liked your piece on [full H1]” and “You invite ideas and stories.”
- Every first-touch email includes the guide URL.
- Do not call it **Parent's Guide to ZPD** on a school or P&C page. Say the tutoring-fit guide, or the post title.
- Sign-off: **Jacques** on P&C / school. `Jacques, ZPD Learning` only on blogs/resource sites that already talk to businesses.
- Do not put “Happy to…” in every email in a pack.
- Newsletter asks go to president/communications/editor — never `admin.officer@`, canteen, accounts.
- P&C / “share a story” = template **4**, even if they have a Resources URL.
- Wellbeing-only lists (Headspace, Beyond Blue, drug talks) and paid tutor directories = skip, not template 2.

## 1. Blog / parenting site (guest post or link)

**When:** They publish parent articles and invite submissions. Not a tuition directory.

**Subject:** Idea for [Site name]?

**A**

```
Hi [Name if public, else Hi,],

I'm Jacques from ZPD Learning. I enjoyed [named piece] — [one concrete detail from it].

We have a short parent page on whether tutoring is the right next step. If it fits your readers, you're welcome to link it — or I can send a short piece if you'd rather run it as copy.

https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit

Jacques
ZPD Learning
```

**B** — same line 1, then the short piece already written (see `ab-templates-review.md`). They can run it as-is.

## 4. P&C / P&F (community note — not a paid newsletter)

**When:** School P&C, P&F, parent club. Always 4, not 2. Not the school office; not Schoolzine ad sales.

**To:** president@, communications@, or named comms convenor — not admin/ops, not the school’s paid-newsletter inbox.

**Subject:** For P&C members weighing tutoring?

**A**

```
Hi [Name],

I'm Jacques from ZPD Learning. [School] parents may be choosing a tutor this year.

We wrote a short page to help them tell whether that's actually the right next step. If you have notes that go out to members, I wondered whether you might share it.

https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit

Jacques
```

**B** — same line 1, then a ready-to-paste community-notes item, plus the PNG.

**Inline and attach (A and B):** `phase-1-quick-wins/guide-assets/is-tutoring-the-right-fit-newsletter.png`.

## 5. Public school office (T5) — get into the newsletter

**When:** Published school inbox (office / admin / info). Prefer a published comms or marketing inbox if one exists.

**Open:** `Dear [ACRONYM] team,` — acronym as the school uses it. Not Hi. Not Dear Sir/Madam.

**Ask:** name yourself, then a soft newsletter ask. Do not offer to pay. Do not ask who sells the slot.

If they reply with a rate card, paying is fine. First-touch does not mention money.

**Subject:** For the parent newsletter?

**A**

```
Dear [ACRONYM] team,

I'm Jacques from ZPD Learning. We wrote a short parent page on how to tell whether tutoring is the right next step — too hard, too easy, or about right.

I wondered whether it might be a useful item for your parent newsletter.

https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit

Jacques
```

**B** — same line 1, then a ready-to-paste newsletter item, plus the PNG.

**Inline and attach (A and B):** `phase-1-quick-wins/guide-assets/is-tutoring-the-right-fit-newsletter.png`. Do not offer to pay.
