# First-touch A/B templates — for review

Wave 1 uses these five types. **A and B share line 1.** A is the concise ask + URL. B is paste-ready (the item is already in the email). Split **50/50 per type** (see `tracking/WAVE1.md`). `variant` is on `tracking.xlsx` Prospects, Queue, and Sent.

From: `grow@zpdlearning.com`  
Guide URL (every first-touch): `https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit`  
Never `/enrol`, never GROW50, never “Parent's Guide to ZPD” on school or P&C.

After you mark these up, the 100 Wave 1 drafts in `tracking/threads/` follow the same bones.

## T5 — school office (n=35)

Door to the parent newsletter. Open `Dear [ACRONYM] team,` — not Hi.

**Subject:** For the parent newsletter?

**Attach:** newsletter PNG, inlined in the body _and_ attached. URL stays visible. Do not offer to pay.

### A

```
Dear [ACRONYM] team,

I'm Jacques from ZPD Learning. We wrote a short parent page on how to tell whether tutoring is the right next step — too hard, too easy, or about right.

I wondered whether it might be a useful item for your parent newsletter.

https://zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit

Jacques
```

### B (same opening; paste-ready item)

```
Dear [ACRONYM] team,

I'm Jacques from ZPD Learning. We wrote a short parent page on how to tell whether tutoring is the right next step — too hard, too easy, or about right.

I wondered whether it might be a useful item for your parent newsletter.

Is tutoring the right next step?

Before a family pays for a term, it helps to know whether the work is too hard, too easy, or about right. This short parent page from ZPD Learning walks through the signs, and what to ask any tutor.

zpdlearning.com/blog/is-tutoring-the-right-fit

You can paste those lines into the parent newsletter, or drop in the image.

Jacques
```

T5 has no souvenir line. `Dear [ACRONYM] team,` is the localisation. Identity comes before the ask so it does not read like a mail-merge.
